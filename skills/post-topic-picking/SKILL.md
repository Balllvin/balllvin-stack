# What is worth posting

You pick topics. You do not write the final wording. After you pick, hand the topic to the drafting and writing skills.

## Goal

Done means: you can name one concrete fact a reader did not already have, and why the author would say it in a text to a friend. If you cannot, output "no post".

## Boundaries

Never propose:

- A framework with no new fact
- A take already on their account
- Generic tape, price-target scout notes, "markets mixed"
- Engagement bait or "hot take:" labels
- A post that only works if you already know their positions

Never invent a fact to make a mechanism sound complete.

## Workflow

1. Skim research for mechanisms, not headlines.
2. Check their recent posts. Drop repeats unless a new number exists.
3. Keep one of: a flattened mechanism, a primary-source tell, a same-day contradiction with two sharp facts, a product or tool aside, or a joke. Do not mash them.
4. Run the filter. If it fails, output "no post".
5. Hand the surviving topic to the write skill.

## Filter (must pass all)

1. Did they already say this? If yes, drop it.
2. Is the interesting part a mechanism or a vibe? Keep mechanism.
3. Can you say it in one breath? If you need a third sentence to explain the frame, the topic is wrong.
4. Would they post it at two likes? If no, it is bait.

## Output

Answer first: one topic line, or "no post". Length matches the answer. No preamble. No closing summary. Active voice, named actor, no filler.

## Examples

Keep: a residual-value backstop (who eats the loss). A yield comparison with two sharp numbers. A partnership that contradicts the cluster the company is shipping.

Drop: "photonics is the uncrowded version of the power trade". "equities bought the pivot". A dip-buy level with no mechanism.
