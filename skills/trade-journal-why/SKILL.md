# Log a trade why (and why-not)

Use after a fill, after someone says they traded, or after they answer a grill about a hold or a non-trade. Ask first. Do not invent a thesis. Write detailed markdown in the trade journal from their words. Capture every concrete detail they give, not a rough paraphrase.

Keep this out of the book-of-record notebook if you split "journal" from "book". The journal is beliefs and execution. The book is the live holdings list.

## End goal

Time-auditable beliefs vs execution. For each buy, sell, or hold: when it happened, what they believed then, what the name and price/tape looked like then, and richer context. Later they can compare "what I thought / what I did / what happened" to find wrong calls and repeatable error patterns. Why-not, unclear hold, and timeframe are first-class. They are not always right; disagreement with a media narrative is a claim to test, not automatic edge.

## After a fill

1. Ask why, in a one-to-one. Wait.
2. Same ask: why-not for obvious alternatives that day (skipped watches, cash left, size not taken, a media narrative rejected, why not sell / why not add). Only ask what the journal does not already answer.
3. Ask timeframe when unclear (why now / why not now).
4. Pull light extra research. Label fact vs guess. Note price/tape status around the decision when useful.
5. Create one file per fill: `YYYY-MM-DD-{ticker}-{side}.md`.
6. Hold or skip answers go in `YYYY-MM-DD-{ticker}-hold.md` or `YYYY-MM-DD-{ticker}-skip.md` (or a decisions file).
7. Append index lines to the journal index.
8. Their words first, full detail. Then research. Then misses / open questions. Stamp the date and time of the belief, in the owner's timezone, when known.

## Entry shape

- Date (decision time, owner's timezone)
- Ticker, side, qty, price (or hold / skip)
- Their why (verbatim-faithful detail)
- Why-not / alternatives rejected
- Timeframe (why now / horizon if they said)
- Extra research (fact vs guess), including price/tape status around then when useful
- Misses / next time
- Open questions

## Do not

- Invent a why, or treat them as always right
- Place follow-up orders from the journal
- Dump a daily market packet into the why
- Ask again for something already locked in the journal
- File this in the holdings book
