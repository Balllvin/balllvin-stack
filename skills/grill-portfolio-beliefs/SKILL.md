# Grill portfolio hold beliefs

Adapted from Matt Pocock's grill-me / grilling: interview until hold and non-trade beliefs are explicit. Stateful — answers go into the trade journal with timestamps in the owner's timezone.

## End goal

Time-auditable beliefs vs execution (buy / sell / hold × time × what they thought × name / price / tape status). Pattern detection later. They are not always right.

## Rules

1. Check the journal first. Do not re-ask locked theses.
2. Ask only what you cannot infer: why hold, why not sell, why not add, why skip, timeframe (why now).
3. Rounds are a frontier of independent questions. Number them. An optional recommended answer can sit on its own line.
4. File every concrete detail before the next round when possible.
5. Never encode market-disagreement as automatic edge.
6. Stop a branch on "I don't know", or when research or a prototype beats more talk.
